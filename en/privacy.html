Contact and scope

This policy covers blackstackstudio.com and enquiries to BlackStack Studio through @blackstackstudio_bot, @blackstack_manager2 and our work email. Contact blackstackst@gmail.com for privacy, access and deletion requests.

Data we handle

GitHub Pages processes network information, including IP addresses, browser information, time and requested resources, to deliver and protect the website. The site does not load Google Analytics, advertising pixels or a unique visitor counter.

When you contact us, we receive your message, chosen contact details and attachments. The Telegram bot also receives your Telegram name and identifier, username if available, selected services, language and entry page. Enquiry drafts are saved so you can continue. Voice messages may be transcribed on the studio server; the original recording remains available in Telegram.

Purposes and legal grounds

Enquiry data is used to reply, prepare an estimate, take steps you request before a contract and deliver agreed work. Legitimate interests may apply to business representatives, organising work and preventing misuse. Records may also be required by law. Where GDPR applies, these grounds are in Article 6(1)(b), (f) and (c). Optional preferences, enquiry source measurement and YouTube loading are based on your permission, which you can withdraw.

Recipients and providers

The website uses GitHub Pages. Enquiries use Telegram, Google work email and the bot server hosted by Hetzner. After you explicitly submit an enquiry, the bot delivers it to responsible team members, Polina and Dmitrii. Project specialists may receive materials needed for their work. YouTube loads only with separate permission. Providers may process data in different countries under their own policies, linked below. This policy does not promise that all data stays in one country.

Retention and deletion

Your privacy choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. With permission, the chosen language is remembered within that period. An Instagram source label lasts for up to 24 hours in the current tab. See the cookie policy for details.

Retention of correspondence, drafts, enquiries and project materials depends on answering your request, continuing agreed work, fulfilling contracts and applicable recordkeeping or legal claims. The website does not automatically delete Telegram or email conversations. You can request deletion from the team. Deletion from our working records does not delete independent copies held by Telegram, an email provider or a message recipient; backups and legally required records may have separate retention periods.

Choices and rights

Optional features are off by default. Refusing them does not prevent browsing, playing locally hosted videos or contacting us. Change your choices in the footer of any page. Withdrawal stops further use of the relevant feature and clears its settings in this browser.

You can request access, a copy, correction or deletion and, where applicable, restriction, portability or objection. Proportionate identity verification may be needed. Applicable law determines deadlines and exceptions; the usual GDPR response period is one month. You may complain to a competent data protection authority. This policy does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

External links and updates

Telegram, Instagram, YouTube and email services open through your action and apply their own policies. Website fonts, images and local videos load from our domain. We will update this policy and seek fresh permission where needed if purposes or optional technologies change.
