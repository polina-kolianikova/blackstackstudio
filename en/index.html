What you'll ask three minutes into the call

Answered upfront

How much does it cost?+

Less than an agency with a hundred juniors, and more than a freelancer with a fighting cat. Exact number after a 30-minute brief.

How fast can you ship?+

30 days to production for an MVP. 4–10 weeks for medium complexity. If the task is simple, we ship in a day. Release date is locked on the first call.

Only two lead founders. Can you handle a big project?+

We can. Two lead founders own the product and communication, while a large specialist team joins the build: design, frontend, backend, DevOps, AI, QA, content, and support.

What if I need it urgently?+

Rush means +40%, and we move the queue. Almost always doable. Reply to brief in one hour. Production in 30 days.

NDA and code ownership?+

Standard NDA at brief stage. All code is yours from the first commit. You have permanent repo access.
