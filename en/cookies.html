Cookies and storage policy

Updated 27 November 2026

PrivacyCookiesTerms

Your choice

You do not have to allow optional features to browse or contact us. Choose necessary only, allow optional features or select individual purposes. Continuing to browse or closing settings is not consent. Change or withdraw your choice through Privacy settings in the footer.

Necessary storage

blackstack.privacy in localStorage records the version, time and your choice for each purpose for 180 days. It contains no unique visitor identifier. It is needed to respect your decision. Normal browser caching delivers resources. The website does not set its own HTTP cookies for advertising or visitor counting.

Remembering language

With separate permission, blackstack.language in localStorage remembers your language for up to 180 days within the lifetime of your privacy choice. Without permission, explicit page addresses and browser preferences determine the language, and switching still works. The legacy bs.lang key is cleared when you refuse this purpose.

Enquiry source

With permission, bs_instagram_attribution_v1 in sessionStorage contains only the fixed instagram_profile label and visit time. It expires after 24 hours or when the tab closes. It may be included in internal and bot links to identify the source of an enquiry. Without permission, this feature does not store or add the label to an enquiry.

YouTube

Posters and local videos work without connecting to YouTube. The youtube-nocookie.com player loads when you open a video only if you allow this purpose. You can allow it in the player or open the video directly on YouTube. Google receives network data and may use its own storage technologies; their details and lifetimes are governed by Google. Withdrawal removes the loaded player from our page. Independent Google data can be managed through Google and browser settings.

Storage restrictions and changes

The website works when browser storage is blocked. Your choice may then last only on the current page, so the request can appear again after navigation. Clearing site data resets preferences. New purposes require a new choice; permission does not automatically cover new advertising or analytics services.
